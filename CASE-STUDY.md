# Case Study: The Juniper Table
### A restaurant website and online-ordering experience

**Project type:** Concept website / front-end demonstration  
**Industry:** Food service and hospitality  
**Brand studio:** QORVI Studio (working brand name)  
**Status:** Interactive front-end prototype

## Project overview

The Juniper Table is a fictional restaurant concept created to demonstrate how a restaurant can present its menu, communicate its personality and guide customers through a simple online-ordering journey.

The visual direction is bold modern: warm neutral backgrounds, confident editorial typography, dark navigation and strong orange accents. The menu combines Nigerian favourites with familiar continental dishes.

## The brief

Create a restaurant website that feels distinctive rather than template-driven, works across desktop and mobile screens, and lets visitors explore food and complete a realistic sample order.

## The experience

- **Restaurant landing page:** A prominent hero, brand message and food photography introduce the restaurant.
- **Menu discovery:** Visitors can browse menu categories and scan dish descriptions and prices.
- **Shopping bag:** Customers can add items, change quantities, remove items and see the subtotal update.
- **Checkout:** A form collects sample customer details and lets the visitor choose delivery or pickup.
- **Order confirmation:** A generated demo reference and order summary provide a clear end to the journey.

## Design decisions

- A warm, restrained colour palette keeps the food photography in focus.
- Large serif headlines create an editorial identity, balanced by clean sans-serif interface text.
- Repeated menu cards and clear category controls make a varied menu easier to browse.
- The shopping bag remains accessible while browsing.
- Mobile layouts give the menu, bag and checkout room to work on narrow screens, with larger touch targets and scrollable panels.

## What the prototype demonstrates

The project demonstrates a complete front-end ordering flow, from menu discovery through to a sample order confirmation. Cart quantities and totals update in the browser, and the checkout experience responds to delivery or pickup selection.

## Scope and limitations

This is a **front-end demonstration**, not a live restaurant system.

- No payment is collected.
- Orders are not sent to a restaurant or stored in a production database.
- Customer details entered into the demo are not submitted to a live ordering service.
- Menu items, prices, contact details, opening hours and restaurant information are fictional placeholders.
- Food images are illustrative and include third-party sources. Their individual licence and reuse permissions must be checked before any commercial deployment.

A production version would need a secure backend, order storage and management, payment-provider integration, delivery rules, notifications, and restaurant-managed menu and availability data. It should also use restaurant-owned or appropriately licensed food photography.

## Outcome

The result is a responsive restaurant website prototype with an interactive menu, cart, checkout and order-confirmation experience. It can be used to demonstrate the proposed customer journey to a restaurant owner and as a starting point for a properly scoped client project.
